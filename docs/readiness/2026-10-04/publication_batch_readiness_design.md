Raw capture was not available to this reviewer. 8. Produce deterministic prospective source-supported batches, initially at most ten whole targets per batch. They are work-planning batches, not executable selections; all rows have `executable: false`, `publication_approved: false` and explicit unresolved gates. The object kind/schema must differ from `safe_to_upload`, record QA and release manifests.

A compact row needs: target ID/kind; complete source members and original-file inventory; measured semantic status and evidence pins; optional exact prepared metadata/artifact references with binding status; historical/current identity evidence and all conflicts; proposed nonexecuting disposition; blocker list; deterministic batch ID. Top-level input pins, source/target/file accounting and preserved protected identities are enough. A second duplicate ledger or general workflow engine is unnecessary.

**Optional restart evidence**

An explicitly supplied, strictly typed sanitized snapshot may produce informational hints using existing states. Missing/malformed or stale bindings stay held. Uncertain create or a write intent without a proven outcome means “reconcile before retry.” A known same-draft partial upload may mean “resume same draft after validated readback,” never “create another.” A verified matching published record means preserve/no-create. Every hint remains nonexecutable, retains all original IDs/DOIs and binds the exact source, prepared metadata and artifact hashes.

Do not infer safe creation from a missing local registry row. Production singleton code lacks the sandbox canary's separate consumed-create grant if the registry itself is lost. The new planner need not solve that live recovery issue, but must report it honestly. With no sanitized snapshot supplied, one explicit `restart_evidence_unavailable` status is sufficient.

**Actual live work remains separate**

Ordinary batching currently calls the legacy `/api/deposit/depositions` transport. The modern modules are sealed fictional sandbox controllers; `modern_draft_schema.synthetic_payload` does not provide a general source-record mapper or publisher decision. No transport change or interpretation of the parent's current Mac diagnostic is needed for this plan.

Enabling class mutations later requires an explicit class-capable draft adapter and durable target-level recovery binding; two-file resume/readback; production record/concept/version/DOI reconciliation and preservation; class-aware QA, duplicate/program review and release; and proof that both aliases cannot also upload. That is more than a manifest change. Do not create an escape flag in the singleton guard or route a pair through one arbitrarily selected member.
